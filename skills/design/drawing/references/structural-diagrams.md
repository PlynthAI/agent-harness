# Structural diagrams

For teaching plates, structural concept illustrations, assemblies, load paths,
connection families, exploded systems and matched precise and expressive series.

## Start with the teaching relationship

State the one relationship the plate must make visible before choosing a medium.
Build a semantic skeleton from only the elements the lesson needs: supports,
spans, vertical and lateral elements, surfaces, joints, forces, reactions and the
path between them.

Keep geometry fictional unless the source is verified. Never imply code
compliance, capacity, safe spans, constructability or a resolved connection.
Arrows and accent marks are evidence: every path ends at a member or support that
can receive it. Keep lecture prose, dimensions and callout numbers outside the
drawing unless asked.

Three.js suits depth, occlusion, axonometric assemblies, exploded layers and
repeated spatial frames; p5.js suits plan and elevation fields, flat taxonomies
and hand-drawn 2D systems; brush media add pressure, smudge, grain, charcoal
weight or blue-pencil emphasis when that helps the lesson.

## Concept grammar

- **Load paths:** an applied force, a receiving sequence, a reaction - typically
  surface, secondary member, primary member, vertical element, foundation.
  Relative direction and hierarchy only; no calculated values without verified
  source.
- **Lateral stability:** the point of lateral entry, the diaphragm or collector,
  the vertical resisting system and the ground; frames, walls and braces kept
  visibly distinct.
- **Spans:** both supports visible; depth, repetition or triangulation express
  relative behaviour, not performance. In an exploded view keep layers registered
  so one unit traces from top to ground.
- **Grids and repeated systems:** keep rhythm and alignment; a removed bay is
  absent, not faded.
- **Connections:** show the members and the bearing or transfer interface; a
  fastener, plate, notch, weld or adhesive plane only when it is the lesson; one
  projection and scale across a taxonomy.

## Paired analytical and expressive plates

Freeze the concept and learning outcome, the semantic relationships, the canvas,
seed and bounds, the accent's meaning, the claim boundary and provenance before
drawing either. Let the medium change the reading, not the proposition.

- **Analytical (Three.js):** orthographic or controlled axonometric camera, matte
  paper and neutral materials with soft shadow, the accent reserved for the
  active path, bay or interface, transparency only to reveal a hidden relation,
  repeated systems exact.
- **Expressive (p5.js and brush):** skeleton before texture; separate paper,
  tone, structure, dust, erasure and accent passes; charcoal from a soft body,
  one decisive edge and sparse dust; the accent as a teaching mark, not
  decoration.

Review each pair: can each plate teach alone; do both keep the same support,
span, path, joint or bay; does the spatial version add understanding; does the
expressive one add energy without hiding the system, and stay architectural at
lecture scale? Inspect every plate at full size, lecture scale and thumbnail.
