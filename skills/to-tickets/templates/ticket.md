# <Ticket title>

**Outcome:** What becomes true, and why it matters.

**Scope and constraints:** Relevant boundaries, non-goals and accepted decisions.

**Context:** Links to the request, specification, decision or parent issue.

**Spec section:** The heading in the linked specification this ticket delivers.

**Acceptance:** Observable behavior or evidence that establishes completion.

**Done:** What must hold for the ticket to be finished, including where it lands.

**Delivery acceptance:** How the landed or deployed change is confirmed.

**Blocked by:** Actual dependencies, or none.

Use the project's status and ownership fields where required. Omit fields that
add no information, and use its established template instead when available.
