# Optional handoff assessment

Use when the requested handoff crosses an ownership boundary or needs a readiness
judgment. This assessment reads existing review evidence; it does not add a reviewer
or rerun code-review's push sequence.
This guidance consolidates the former `sdlc-review` method.

Compare the deliverable with the request, any relevant local plan, and existing
checks and review findings. Verify that evidence still describes the delivered
contents and relevant conditions; a commit or push alone does not expire it.
Name missing, stale or failed required evidence rather than rerunning the review
pair or manufacturing a pass. Optional paperwork and harmless plan differences
are not blockers.

Return a short judgment in the existing handoff or task record:

- **Ready:** the requested outcome and required evidence are present.
- **Changes needed:** identify the concrete gap, smallest repair and owner.
- **Decision needed:** identify the unresolved choice and who has authority.

Include the candidate, evidence relied on, remaining limits and next action only
as needed for the recipient to proceed. No additional receipt, certificate or
fixed schema is required. Readiness is not the merge gate and grants no deployment or
hosted approval authority.
