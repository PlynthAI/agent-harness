# Marketing and email

Resolve the audience, purpose, channel and desired reader action. Start from
the project's existing voice, examples, audience guidance and approved claims;
those are project facts, not a universal brand style. Preserve the requested
format or template when one exists.

Make one clear promise or point at a time. Support material product, numeric or
comparative claims with approved project evidence or current sources. If a
claim cannot be supported, qualify it or leave it out rather than inventing
proof. Keep the subject or headline, body and call to action consistent with
the same offer and audience.

For an email, state the action plainly and make links or dates accurate. For
marketing copy, align the copy with the actual product or campaign brief and
use the project's SEO or channel guide when relevant. Present alternatives
only when the user asks for options or a meaningful choice remains open.
