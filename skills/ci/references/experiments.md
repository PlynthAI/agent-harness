# Measure one CI change

Define the comparison before collecting treatment results. A compact section in the
existing finding is sufficient.

Record the hypothesis, actual bottleneck, control/treatment source and workflow,
selected check/test identities, runner/runtime/lockfile/cache conditions, primary
latency or resource outcome, acceptable tradeoffs, finite experiment scope and rollback.
Use established usage authority; ask only about a genuinely missing material scope.

Interleave matched controls and treatments when practical. Distinguish conditions
that must match globally from those that must match within each pair. Preserve failed
comparability checks and all attempted runs in consumption. Do not reassign a favorable
sample, reuse one run as independent replications or amend acceptance after seeing
results without explicitly disclosing the change.

A pull-request head is not proof of its executed synthetic merge. Use actual receipts,
merge parents or checked-out evidence; preserve disagreement with event metadata.
Counts alone do not establish coverage. For reordered concurrent output, compare
available file/suite/test identities as a multiset preserving duplicate multiplicity,
plus skipped/missing coverage; output-order differences need not mean changed tests.

For history reduction, trace imported helpers and historical-object lookups. Probe a
real shallow checkout including a nonadjacent historical branch and necessary ancestry
deepening. Measure fetched objects/data and time, not subprocess count alone.
For caches, include lookup, misses, restore, save and post work. Separate an authenticated
validation receipt from an optional cache used to prepare later work.

Use relevant negative controls for changed behavior: a selected failure still blocks;
selection errors remain conservative; stale identity or malformed evidence does not
pass. Do not add unrelated negative tests or rerun unchanged validation mechanically.

Report matched results with sample size and uncertainty. A local omitted-operation
timing is not a hosted saving; observed job seconds are not billed minutes. Account
for observation and unsuccessful experiment work. Estimate payback only with supported
eligible volume and cost assumptions. A small exploratory sample cannot establish p95.

Keep/reject the change based on correctness and the stated economics. Review through
existing code-review and deliver through the project's normal route. Record the new
critical path and verify ordinary subsequent runs. Rollback must remain compatible
with the project's trusted policy; it cannot bypass a required admission transition.
